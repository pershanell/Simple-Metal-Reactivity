Experiment 2 — Does plate area change voltage or current?

Choose a pair from zinc, copper and nickel. Change the exposed area of both plates together, keeping the electrolyte, temperature, distance between electrodes and electrode materials unchanged.

Before measuring, predict what will happen when the plate area increases. A simple classroom model assumes voltage is independent of area, while current increases with area under the same load. Real current also depends on electrode kinetics, solution resistance and mass transport.
